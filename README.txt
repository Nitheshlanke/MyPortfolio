NITHESH LANKE — CHEF PORTFOLIO

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the nithesh_chef_portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge or Firefox recommended).

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Responsive 3D-style chef portfolio website
- Your two supplied chef photographs in assets/
- 20 dish cards with ingredients and preparation steps
- Dish filters: Continental, Bakery and Desserts
- Recipe pop-up, mobile navigation and 3D hover effects

IMPORTANT
- Internet is required for the Google Fonts and dish photographs (Unsplash).
- Your chef photos are stored locally in assets/, so they remain available offline.
- Review all resume details, job titles, dates, certificates and contact details before publishing.
- Dish recipes are portfolio sample recipes; adjust them to your actual experience and kitchen standards.
- The passport, CDC, SID and certificate numbers from your supplied text were intentionally not published on the public portfolio for privacy and security.
- To edit text or recipes, open index.html in VS Code and search for the relevant section or dish name.
